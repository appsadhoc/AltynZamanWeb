Founded in 2019, Altyn Zaman Ishewurlik Merkezi Economic Society specializes in the retail of luxury and premium apparel, footwear, watches, and accessories.

As an authorized dealer, the company represents 16 international luxury and fashion brands in Ashgabat, with boutiques in the Altyn Zaman and Arkach business and shopping centres.

Every item comes directly from the brand and is guaranteed original.
